Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a creator?

Sign up for a free account and go to your dashboard. From there you can create your first story in minutes — no technical skills required.

How does monetization work?

You set the price for each story. Scouties takes a small platform commission, and you receive a revenue share every time a player purchases your story.

Is there a minimum number of stories to publish?

No minimum required. Publish at your own pace — even a single story can attract players and generate income.

Which cities are available?

Scouties works in any city worldwide. If yours isn't listed yet, you'll be the first creator there — a great opportunity to dominate a new market.

How is the creator ranking determined?

Rankings are based on the number of published stories, average rating, and total player count. The more quality stories you publish, the higher your rank.
