Game Route Information

Select 5 to 15 locations to build the route of your game, and add the required information for each of these.

Learn more

As you build your route, make sure that the distances between checkpoints do not exceed 550m (1800 feet).

The first location of your game's route is known as the 'Starting point'.

Players will need to stand at the starting point in order for the game to begin.
